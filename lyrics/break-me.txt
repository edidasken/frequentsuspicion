[Verse 1]
Father, I’ve got nothing left to bargain.
Nothing left to prove.
Nothing left worth holding
If it keeps me far from You.
Strip away the counterfeit.
Burn away my pride.
Leave no hidden corner
Where this old man still survives.
I’ve trusted my own wisdom.
I’ve crowned myself before.
I’ve built a hundred kingdoms…
They all ended on the floor.
So take these empty hands, Lord.
I’ve finally learned they’re Yours.
Whatever You must take from me…
I don’t want it anymore.

[Build]
Break every idol.
Break every chain.
Break every kingdom
Built in my name.
Break every motive
That isn’t true.
Until what’s left…
Looks like You.

[Chorus]
Break me, Lord!
Break me completely!
If this heart won’t bend…
Then shatter it deeply!
Take me down into the valley.
Lead me through refining flames.
Mold me with the Potter’s hands.
Until I never look the same.
Not my will…
But Yours alone.
Build Your Kingdom from these bones.

[Verse 2]
Put me in the furnace.
If the fire makes me clean.
Every scar I’ve ever carried…
Use it for Your glory.
Every failure. Every secret.
Every tear I’ve tried to hide.
Every room inside this heart…
Throw the doors open wide.
Teach me how to kneel again.
Teach me how to simply stay.
Teach me faith that doesn’t move
When everything gives way.
If obedience costs everything…
Then everything is cheap.
Compared to hearing, “Well done…”
When eternity speaks.

[Build]
Lead me.
Guide me.
Correct me.
Humble me.
Search me.
Know me.
Nothing hidden.
Only Thee.

[Breakdown]
If You have to…

Break…
Everything…
BREAK IT!

If You have to…
Burn…
Everything…
BURN IT!

If You have to…
Take…
Everything…
TAKE IT!

Just don’t…
Leave me…
The same.

[Bridge]
The clay doesn’t question…
The hands of the Potter.
The branch doesn’t argue…
With the Gardener’s knife.
The cross wasn’t comfortable…
But it became my freedom.
So shape me through suffering…
And breathe Your life into mine.

[Final Chorus]
Break me, Lord!
Break what keeps me from You!
Mold me, Lord!
Make my spirit brand new!
Lead me through the furnace.
Refine me in the flame.
Let every shattered piece of me…
Bring glory to Your name.
Take the wreckage of my story.
Write redemption on every page.
Until my life becomes a testimony…
Of amazing grace.

[Outro]
Your will…
Not mine.
Your hands…
Not mine.
Your glory…
Not mine.

Amen.
