[Verse 1]
I don't see the world the way I used to see, there's a crack in the glass looking back at me. Every quiet room has a hidden door, every sudden sound has a little more war. I read every face like a coded threat, hear words people never even said. I watch for the shift, for the change in the air, for the shadow moving that nobody knows is there. I don't mean to flinch, I don't mean to pull away, but my body learned fear in a permanent way. Even when the sun lays gold on the ground, I'm still listening hard for the incoming sound.

[Verse 2]
Love can look like danger when your heart's been trained, peace can feel suspicious when you're used to pain. A soft hand reaches and I still brace, like kindness might turn with a different face. I've mistaken calm for the eye of the storm, mistaken silence for the shape of war. I've stood in safe rooms with my back to the wall, still waiting for the next thing to fall. People say, "You're home now, let it go," like time can command what the body knows. But some men come back with a map in their skin, trying to find the way out again.
