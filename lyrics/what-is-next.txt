[Chorus]
Lord, show me what is next.
I have nothing left to claim.
I can’t undo the years behind me.
I can’t take away their pain.
If there’s purpose in repentance
After everything I’ve wrecked,
Take whatever life is left in me.
Lord, show me what is next.

[Verse 1]
I inherited chaos, then passed on the pain.
Swore I would end it, then gave it a name.
Carried old wars into rooms meant for peace.
Called it protection while fear spoke through me.
Two vows lie broken behind where I stand.
Two homes bear the marks of who I am.
My daughters learned burdens they never should know.
I planted the hurt I was desperate not to grow.

I can trace every fracture through blood and through years—
From the wounds that made me to the wounds I put here.
Good intentions don’t heal what my choices have done.
Being sorry today cannot change who I was.
I was wounded, Father…
Then I became a wound.
Now I’m standing in the silence
Of every broken room.

[Build]

You restore, but not always the life that I would.
I’m done asking mercy to hide what I’ve wrecked.
I’m here in the ruins…
Lord, show me what is next.

[Verse 2]
An apology cannot give childhood back.
A changed man can’t erase every memory they have.
And repentance is not making wounded hearts stay
To watch me become who I should have been.
I won’t make my sorrow another weight they must hold.
I won’t ask them to heal me.
Teach me love without access.
Faith without praise.
Teach me to keep changing when no one feels safe.

If they never come nearer, make me trustworthy still.
If I never see healing, keep bending my will.
If the proof takes a lifetime, then give me the years.
Let obedience speak when no audience hears.
I cannot be the father they needed back then.
I cannot return and begin it again.
But I’m still their father—so show me somehow
Who You require me to become for them now.
