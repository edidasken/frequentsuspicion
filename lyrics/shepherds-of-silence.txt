“Shepherds of Silence”

[Intro]  
This is not one house.  
Not one church.  
This is every locked door  
Where truth was taught to whisper.

[Verse 1]  
A wounded man came honest, laid the evidence down,  
While respectable men protected peace in the town.  
Trauma in his bloodstream, no sleep in his head,  
Still he served every Sunday while his spirit went dead.  
He asked for intervention; they offered him a phrase,  
Called endurance holy while the danger stayed in place.  
They heard dates, saw the damage, watched the pattern repeat,  
Then preached about forgiveness from a comfortable seat.  
Listening without action is permission wearing grace;  
Neutrality helps the hand that keeps the victim in his place.

[Chorus]  
Mercy without justice is a shelter for the lie,  
A shepherd who won’t guard the sheep just watches them die.  
Polish every pulpit, keep your Sunday voices sweet—  
Heaven hears the breaking behind every closed street.  
You knew. You knew.  
Now the buried truth comes through.  
When shepherds fail to protect,  
God calls the shepherds too.

[Verse 2]  
A home became a hotel; every boundary disappeared,  
Pain became an insult, every warning went unheard.  
Money burned, trust broke, illness became blame,  
Then the family got a story designed to stain his name.  
“Crazy.” “Out of control.” Make the wounded one the threat;  
Build a wall of friendly faces and call the matter set.  
That is how coercion hides in daylight dressed as care—  
One controls the narrative; the other gasps for air.  
Not a simple disagreement, not two tempers in a fight:  
One keeps taking ground while the crowd calls it “both sides.”

(Repeat Chorus)

[Verse 3]  
A holiday table crowded while one soul sat alone;  
First the smear, then the silence, then the blocking of the phone.  
Doctors saw the danger that the church refused to name,  
But leaders softened cruelty into ordinary shame.  
When he stepped away to live, they called the leaving sin,  
Judged the act of surviving, not the cage that held him in.  
Kept the public image standing while the private warnings climbed,  
Then acted months too late and called the outcome “God’s own time.”  
If it mattered at the ending, it mattered at the start;  
Delay is still a choice when someone’s life is torn apart.

[Bridge]  
Knowledge creates duty.  
Hearing carries weight.  
Neutrality serves the violence  
Every time the shepherds wait.  
Read the whole record,  
Not the smoothest story told.  
A sanctuary without courage  
Is cowardice dressed in gold.

[Breakdown]  
Do not preach forgiveness to erase accountability.  
Do not rename coercion “marital complexity.”  
Do not use a diagnosis to discredit what is true.  
Do not demand repentance from the one who came to you.  
No title outranks justice.  
No pulpit buries proof.  
Mercy that refuses action  
Leaves the wounded as the truth.
