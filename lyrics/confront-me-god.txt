(Verse 1)
Confront me, God. Leave nothing hidden.
Search every motive. Expose what’s forbidden.
Shine through the darkness I’ve learned to defend.
Break every idol I still call a friend.
Pull every mask from the face that I wear.
Strip every lie that I breathe like it’s air.
Cut through the pride that I baptized as strength.
Teach me surrender, whatever the length.
I don’t want comfort if comfort means chains.
I don’t want pleasure if pleasure remains.
Lead me through fire if fire makes me whole.
Nothing is worth what it costs for my soul.

(Build)
Search me.
Know me.
Test every word.
Silence the voices…
So Yours can be heard.
Burn every bridge…
Leading to sin.
If I must lose…
Then let holiness win.

(Chorus)
Confront me, God!
Don’t leave me the same!
Tear down every kingdom
Built in my name!
Break what is broken.
Heal what is true.
If I lose everything…
But I still have You.

Confront me, God!
Whatever it costs.
Better a wounded servant…
Than a comfortable loss.
Teach me obedience.
Teach me to stand.
Write Your commandments…
Deep in my hands.

(Verse 2)
I know temptation don’t knock like a storm.
It whispers until compromise feels warm.
One little secret becomes who I am.
One quiet failure becomes my command.
I don’t want history written again.
I don’t want pride wearing holiness’ skin.
Guard my daughters. Guard my sons.
Teach them battles already won.
Don’t let my failures become their guide.
Let them see Jesus, not my pride.
Take every scar and every shame.
Turn every loss toward Your name.

(Breakdown)
Break me…Before sin breaks me.
Humble me…Before pride takes me.
Correct me…Before judgment finds me.
Never let my own heart…Blind me.

(Bridge)
Search me…O God.
Know my anxious heart.
Test me…O God.
Show me every part.
If there is any wicked way in me…
Don’t leave it there.
Lead me in the everlasting way.
Because I cannot lead myself.

(Final Chorus)
Confront me, God!
Speak and I’ll obey!
Even when Your mercy
Cuts the deepest way.
Take this heart of stone.
Make it beat like Yours.
Finish what You started.
Keep me faithful, Lord.

Confront me, God!
Until nothing remains…
But Christ in me.
Your glory.
Your Kingdom.
Your Name.
