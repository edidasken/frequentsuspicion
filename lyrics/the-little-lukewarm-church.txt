[Intro]
There was a little lukewarm church
That loved the sound of truth—
As long as truth stayed quiet.

[Verse 1]
Little lukewarm church with the welcome sign,
Coffee in the lobby, every sermon right on time.
Hands raised high beneath the gentle light,
Singing “God is good” while avoiding every fight.
They loved grace deeply when grace required nothing,
Called silence “wisdom,” called retreating “loving.”
A wounded soul arrived with the evidence in hand;
They offered vague compassion, too afraid to take a stand.
“We are praying for you”—then they hurried down the hall;
Prayer sounds convenient when you plan to do nothing at all.

[Chorus]
Little lukewarm church, neither burning nor cold,
With a mouth full of Scripture and a backbone sold.
You guarded your comfort, your image, your name,
While somebody was dying and you softened the blame.
Little lukewarm church, Heaven knows what you knew—
You cannot hide in mercy when justice was due.
Truth stood at your doorway;
Courage never came through.

[Verse 2]
Called abuse “conflict,” called coercion a flaw,
Turned a cage into a marriage needing one more talk.
“Both sides need forgiveness”—the respectable escape:
Give the wounded half the blame, give the powerful more space.
You heard about the threats, isolation, and fear,
But the smoother voice was easier for everyone to hear.
When the victim drew a boundary just to stay alive,
You questioned his submission, never why he had to hide.
A shepherd without courage is a hireling in disguise,
Counting every Sunday while the sheep are sacrificed.

[Verse 3]
Little lukewarm church kept the ministry alive,
While the warnings gathered dust and a home broke inside.
Then months behind the moment, when denial could not stand,
You quietly made changes with clean, unbloodied hands.
No confession from the pulpit, no admission you were late—
Just another careful meeting, another polished statement.
Delayed protection is a choice somebody made;
Every silent elder helped the darkness keep its place.
If the truth mattered later, it mattered from the start;
You cannot call it shepherding when fear controls your heart.

[Bridge]
You studied every passage,
But missed the bleeding man.
You preached restoration
Without a righteous stand.
You wanted resurrection
Without facing what was killed.
You wanted peace and mercy—
But never God’s full will.

[Breakdown]
No more holy hesitation.
No more polished alibis.
No more weaponized forgiveness.
No more sheltering the lies.
Repentance bears its fruit.
Protection carries cost.
A church that saves its image
Has already lost the lost.

[Outro]
The doors were open.
The lights were on.
The songs were beautiful.
But the Shepherd stood outside, knocking.
