[Intro]
I thought I was chasing life…
Turns out…I was running from me.

[Verse 1]
I called the garden a prison, called the desert paradise.
Traded truth for a whisper, traded peace to feed my pride.
She said, “Don’t cross this line,” I swore she couldn’t understand.
Now I know God was reaching through the woman holding my hand.
I called conviction control, called mercy a set of chains.
Thought I was breaking free while I was breaking everything.
The devil never made me—I chose my own desire.
I burned the home You gave me just to chase another fire.

[Build]
I mistook warning for rejection.
I mistook patience for restraint.
I mistook love for my enemy…
Now every sunrise bears my name.

[Chorus]
I chose the lie over the truth.
I traded forever for something new.
I broke the hands that carried mine.
Now I live one day at a time.
Seventeen years and the echoes remain.
Every tomorrow still whispers my name.
Some debts are measured in tears, not time…
And I’ve been paying mine.

[Verse 2]
Pictures stood still while the years moved on.
My children grew up while their father was gone.
Every birthday candle, every Christmas light,
Reminds me love was worth another fight.
I chased what glittered, abandoned the gold.
Bought a lie that left my spirit cold.
I was so busy trying to find myself…
I became somebody nobody else could help.

[Breakdown]
I wasn’t trapped.
I wasn’t forced.
I knew the truth…
And chose another course.
I didn’t stumble.
I didn’t drift.
I opened both hands…
And dropped the gift.

[Bridge]
If your heart is wandering… listen before it’s gone.
The voice calling you home may not call for long.
Don’t trade a covenant for a passing flame.
The fire burns out… but the scars remain.

[Final Chorus]
I chose the lie over the truth.
Now I wear the proof.
If one father stays…
If one husband turns around…
Then these ruins become a warning instead of a grave.
The greatest love I ever lost…
Was the one I was already holding.

[Outro]
She wasn’t standing in my way…
She was standing between me…
And the worst decision…of my life.
