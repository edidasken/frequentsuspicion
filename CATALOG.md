# FrequentSuspicion Catalog

## Current production status

The complete catalog contains 46 songs. Published videos and official lyrics are connected to the interactive site as they become available; unfinished chapters retain their album context and use intentional placeholders.

## Album One — The War Came Home

**Playlist title:** The War Came Home: Recognizing the Destruction
**Movement:** The Wound / The Soldier Returns
**Central question:** “Why is the war still here when the war is over?”
**Songs:** 9

1. **The War Came Home:** The realization that the battlefield followed him home. Trauma is no longer overseas; it lives in quiet rooms, relationships, and memories.
2. **When the Night Goes Quiet:** The loneliness of the watchman. When the world sleeps, the mind stays awake.
3. **Shattered Lens:** The way trauma changes perception. Learning that survival skills can also become barriers to peace.
4. **Wounded, But Still Going:** The declaration that being wounded does not mean being defeated.
5. **September in July:** The collision of celebration and trauma. A beautiful moment becomes a doorway into the past.
6. **The Darkest Valley:** Walking through suffering while discovering that the Shepherd is present in the valley.
7. **Sleep Don’t Come Easy:** The body continues fighting after the battle. The struggle between knowing you are safe and feeling safe.
8. **When I Close My Eyes:** The struggle of rest, memory, and facing what appears when everything becomes quiet.
9. **The Man Behind the Startle:** The realization that the reaction is not the identity. There is still a man beyond the trauma response.

## Album Two — The Reckoning

**Playlist title:** The Reckoning: The Story and The Truth
**Movement:** The Story They Told / The Truth Revealed
**Central question:** “Who gets to define who I am?”
**Songs:** 9

1. **Broken Me:** They told me who I was. The battle against the identity others assigned.
2. **The Watchtower:** I saw what was happening. Discernment, isolation, and seeing what others refused to see.
3. **The Life She Wanted:** I saw the story being rewritten. The contrast between the public image and the hidden cost.
4. **Whispers in the Ear:** I saw how the story spread. How narratives travel before truth gets heard.
5. **Stop Casting Stones:** I faced the judgment. The wounded confronting those who judged without understanding.
6. **Your Words Are Echoes:** The words lost their power. The moment accusations stop controlling identity.
7. **Borrowed Throne:** False authority was exposed. The realization that no one gets to take God’s seat.
8. **Your Sunday Best:** The mask. The difference between appearance and reality.
9. **It’s Over:** The release. Leaving the old battlefield and refusing to live there anymore.

## Album Three — The Restoration

**Playlist title:** The Restoration: Death and Rebirth
**Movement:** The Healing / Becoming Whole
**Central question:** “Who am I becoming now?”
**Songs:** 12

1. **The Cycle Ends Here:** I choose differently. The decision that the pattern stops with me.
2. **The Thaw:** I begin to feel again. The frozen places begin to come alive.
3. **Deeper Love:** I understand love differently. Love is no longer disappearance; it is truth and grace.
4. **Back to Me:** I recover who I was meant to be.
5. **Still at the Well:** I know where my source is. Returning to the place where life comes from.
6. **Bring Back the Child in Me:** I return to trust and dependence on the Father.
7. **Don’t Give Me Away Again:** I surrender my wandering heart. The prayer not to be left to myself.
8. **No Waste at the Altar:** I recognize that God redeemed even the wreckage.
9. **I Bow My Knees:** I submit everything back to Him.
10. **You Still Call My Name:** I receive my identity from God instead of people.
11. **By His Grace:** I live from the identity God has given me.
12. **Death Day:** The former versions of me are laid down so I can receive the life God is giving me now.

## Album Four — Carry It Forward

**Subtitle:** From Wreckage to Redemption
**Overall movement:** Confession / Formation / Discernment / Commission
**Central question:** “How can God carry redemption forward through everything that was broken?”
**Songs:** 16
**Catalog status:** Locked

### Act I — The Wreckage

**Movement:** Confession without disguise. The fire is named, the choices are owned, and the heart is brought into the light.

1. **My Dumpster Fire:** Owning the ruin without excuses and discovering that grace can still reach the ashes.
2. **Every Day Was Sin:** Recognizing that the destruction was not one isolated mistake, but a repeated choice made against conviction.
3. **Confront Me, God:** Asking God to expose every hidden motive, idol, lie, and counterfeit refuge.
4. **Break Me:** Surrendering pride, self-rule, and every internal kingdom that competes with God.
5. **A Prayer to The God of My Life:** Releasing vengeance without denying the damage; praying for mercy, truth, accountability, justice, and repentance.

### Act II — The Rebuilding

**Movement:** Surrender becomes formation. God teaches the wounded heart to live, love, remain, and remember its birthright.

6. **Teach Me to Live:** Moving beyond survival, numbness, and isolation and asking God to teach the heart how to become whole.
7. **Mend the Broken Portrait:** Asking God to restore the man, father, and identity beneath the fractured family picture—not merely cover the cracks.
8. **Biblical Love:** Rejecting selfish desire disguised as love and learning the patient, sacrificial love revealed by Christ.
9. **Covenant:** Understanding that covenant is holy faithfulness rather than something governed by changing feelings.
10. **Birthright:** Recognizing the identity and inheritance traded for rebellion and returning to what God originally gave.

### Act III — The Reckoning

**Movement:** Discernment sharpens. Masks fall, silence is confronted, and mercy refuses to abandon justice.

11. **The Many Wolves in Masks:** Learning to recognize false spiritual authority by its fruit rather than its polished language or appearance.
12. **The Little Lukewarm Church:** Confronting a church culture that praises truth but retreats when truth requires courage and action.
13. **Shepherds of Silence:** Holding spiritual leaders accountable when passive listening and institutional comfort leave wounded people unprotected.

### Act IV — The Commission

**Movement:** The inward war becomes a warning, a witness, and a legacy carried beyond the ruins.

14. **The Battle for Your Soul:** Warning that compromise begins with belief and worship, and calling others to guard their hearts.
15. **A Million Times Over:** Fully owning the choices that destroyed what was given and allowing the testimony to become a warning for others.
16. **Carry It Forward:** Asking God to complete His work through the narrator, his family, and future generations so that faithfulness—not failure—becomes the inheritance.
